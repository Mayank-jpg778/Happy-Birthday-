# Happy Birthday Shruti ✨ — Birthday Web App

A luxury birthday web application crafted in a **Red Velvet, Obsidian Black, and Golden** theme, created with all love **From Krish** for **Shruti**.

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Monochrome Black Background with Glass Reflection & Ornate Photo Frame**:
   - Deep obsidian black velvet background with crimson glow.
   - Right-hand side features an ornate golden glowing frame holding Shruti & Krish's couple photo.
   - Realistic glass floor mirror reflection beneath the frame.
   - Engraved golden plaque: *"Shruti & Krish - Forever & Always - From Krish ❤️"*.

2. **Interactive 3D Gifts Unboxing 🎁**:
   - Velvet red, obsidian black, and royal gold wrapped animated gift boxes with 3D bows and ribbons.
   - Click to open with 3D lid lift, sparkling chimes, and particle burst.
   - Secret reveals:
     - *"You are the cutest in the world! 🥰"*
     - *"Dark truth: You are undeniably loveliest! 💖"*
     - *"May your dreams sparkle brighter! ✨"*

3. **Envelope & Birthday Wish Card 💌**:
   - 3D crimson velvet envelope sealed with a golden wax seal: *"This for you Shruti 💌"*.
   - Clicking opens the envelope flap and slides out a heartfelt letter.
   - Automatically bursts birthday crackers & golden fireworks across the screen.
   - Plays the "Happy Birthday to you" song!

4. **Realistic Red Velvet Cake Cutting Ceremony 🎂**:
   - Multi-tier red velvet cake with gold pearls, frosting drips, and 5 burning candles with animated flickering flames.
   - Gold plaque topper: *"Happy Birthday Shruti ✨"*.
   - Interactive *"Cut The Cake, Shruti! 🎂"* button.
   - Slicing animation with golden knife, slice separates, candles blow out.
   - Plays celebration fanfare and birthday song.
   - Screen floods with golden fireworks, floating hearts, and burst confetti!

5. **Photo & Video Memories with Captions & Lightbox**:
   - All 7 uploaded photos and 4 videos curated with cute, personalized captions celebrating her beauty, smile, and memories.
   - Filter tabs: All Memories, Cute Photos, Memory Videos.
   - Fullscreen lightbox modal with emoji reaction buttons (❤️, 👑, ✨, 🥰) that float animated hearts.
   - Interactive Memory Slideshow with auto-play, countdown progress bar, thumbnail carousel, and fullscreen mode.

6. **Music and Sound Effects 🎵**:
   - Self-contained Web Audio API synthesizer for 100% offline reliability.
   - "Happy Birthday" melody (chime/music box), cracker pops, sparkle chimes, and celebration fanfare.
   - Ambient BGM play/pause toggle button in the header.

   Double-click `index.html` or open `file:///C:/Users/HP/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/shruti-birthday/index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Firefox.

2. **Using a Local Server (Optional)**:
   In your terminal inside `C:\Users\HP\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\shruti-birthday`:
